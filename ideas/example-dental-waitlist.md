# Idea brief

## One-sentence pitch
Independent dental clinics losing revenue to same-day cancellations can fill the slot within minutes by
texting an opted-in waitlist, instead of the front desk phoning patients one by one.

## Target actor and context (hypothesis)
- Actor / role: practice manager or owner-dentist of a 1-3 chair independent clinic
- Company type / size / geography: independent (non-chain) clinics, Germany and UK to start
- Situation that triggers the need: a cancellation inside 24 hours leaves an unbillable chair hour

## Job to be done
- "Fill this empty slot today without tying up my receptionist for an hour."

## Current workaround / status quo and what it costs them
- Receptionist calls down a paper or spreadsheet list; often 30-60 minutes of phone time; many slots stay empty.

## Why now
- Practice-management systems expose booking APIs; SMS is cheap; post-2024 staffing shortages make front-desk time scarce.

## Why us
- Founder's family runs a clinic (access to 5 pilot practices and the local dental association newsletter).

## Candidate verticals / segments to consider
- Dental, physiotherapy, dermatology, veterinary, hair salons, driving schools

## Business model hypothesis
- Value metric: per clinic per month, flat
- Price point guess: EUR 79-149 / month
- Channel today: dental association newsletter, practice-management-software marketplaces, founder's network

## Day-one profitability target
- Monthly cost floor to run this (EUR): 250
- Founder time available per week: 15 hours
- Customers needed to cover the floor (back-of-envelope): 3 at EUR 99/month

## Known risks / open questions
- Incumbent practice software may already bundle waitlist SMS; patient consent rules; integration effort per PMS.

## Out of scope
- Full online booking, payments, chains with central scheduling.
