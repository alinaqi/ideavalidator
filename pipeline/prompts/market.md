## Your tasks for this stage
Apply the PDLC Market & Competitor lenses. Market research clarifies alternatives, market pull,
constraints and strategic options — it must not become a feature-copy list (PD-C019).
1. **Alternatives / status quo map**: everything the target actor does instead today — direct
   competitors, adjacent tools, agencies/freelancers, spreadsheets, "do nothing". For each: name,
   type (direct / adjacent / service / diy / status_quo), pricing (from their pricing page, with URL),
   positioning, target segment, visible weaknesses (from reviews), and traction signals.
2. **Market pull**: behavioural evidence buyers urgently want this — funding into the category,
   procurement/RFPs, job postings for roles that own this, growth of incumbents, waitlists.
3. **Bottom-up market sizing**: number of target actors (sourced) × plausible annual spend → SAM;
   then a realistic **year-1 obtainable share for a solo/bootstrapped founder** (SOM). Show the
   arithmetic and label every input SOURCED or ESTIMATE. Top-down TAM figures from analyst press
   releases are low-quality; use them only as a sanity check.
4. **Trends / why now**: technology frontier (what just became feasible or cheap), regulation,
   behaviour shifts, platform moves by adjacent giants (threat and opportunity).
5. **Regulatory / policy constraints** that shape adoption (data, licensing, procurement).
6. **Distribution channels** available to a founder *today* without paid ads: communities,
   marketplaces/app stores, partners, SEO gaps, outbound lists, content. For each: founder-accessible
   (yes/no), cost, expected effort, evidence it works in this category.
7. **Whitespace**: underserved segments, unmet jobs, pricing gaps, delivery-model gaps found in reviews.
8. **Learn, don't copy**: 3-5 principles from competitors' successes/failures.

## Required sections
alternatives, market_pull, market_sizing, trends, technology_frontier, regulation,
distribution_channels, whitespace, learn_not_copy

## Scoring criteria
market_pull (behavioural/transactional demand signals), reachable_market_size (SOM supports
profitability for a small team), competitive_whitespace (a defensible unmet gap exists, not just
parity), trend_tailwind (why-now is real and dated), regulatory_feasibility (no blocking constraint
for a small operator).

## Kill flags for this stage
`market_too_small_for_profitability`, `dominant_free_incumbent`, `regulatory_blocker_for_small_operator`,
`no_founder_accessible_channel`
