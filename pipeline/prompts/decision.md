## Your tasks for this stage
Write the decision memo a disciplined bootstrapped founder would act on. Use the PDLC Bet Portfolio
record and Prioritization decision record. Desk research can only ever justify **GO to real-world
testing**, never GO to build.
1. **Verdict**: GO (proceed to the two-week behavioural test plan), PIVOT (change segment, price,
   delivery model or problem — specify which), or KILL. Give the rationale in ≤150 words, citing the
   strongest behavioural evidence and the strongest contrary evidence.
2. **Bet record**: bet title; target outcome (e.g. "3 paying beachhead customers at ≥€X MRR within 60
   days"); opportunity (solution-independent); solution thesis; risk profile (Value/Usability/
   Feasibility/Viability each H/M/L with one line); evidence strength summary (counts of behavioural
   vs stated items, independent sources); expected impact mechanism; cost/effort envelope for the test
   phase; **kill condition** (observable, dated, e.g. "if <2 of 15 ICP conversations commit money by
   day 14"); next evidence to collect.
3. **Pivot options** ranked, each with what evidence already supports it.
4. **Two-week plan**: day ranges, concrete actions (who to contact, where, with what offer), owner,
   output artefact. Day one should produce a revenue-seeking action, not a build task.
5. **Evidence ledger summary** across all stages: counts and the three strongest + three weakest items.
6. **What would change our mind**: observable signals that would flip the verdict either way.
7. **Positioning draft** (April Dunford): competitive alternative, unique attributes, value, best-fit
   customer, market category — one line each, to be tested in outreach copy.

## Required sections
verdict, rationale, bet_record, pivot_options, two_week_plan, evidence_ledger_summary,

## Scoring criteria
evidence_strength (behavioural, fresh, triangulated across stages), profitability_confidence (the
economics stage closes without paid acquisition), focus_clarity (one beachhead, one offer, one
channel), execution_readiness (the founder can start the two-week plan tomorrow with what exists).
