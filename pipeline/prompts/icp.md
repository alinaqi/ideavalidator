## Your tasks for this stage
Narrow to the segment we will validate first. Use Crossing the Chasm beachhead logic, Disciplined
Entrepreneurship market segmentation, and PDLC Empathy & Personas rules (behavioural profiles over
demographic personas; no invented inner life; every trait traceable to evidence).
1. Enumerate **6-10 candidate verticals/segments** (from the brief, problem stage "who feels it most",
   and market whitespace). Include at least two the founder did not list.
2. Score each 0-5 in a **vertical scorecard** on: pain_urgency, budget_authority (does one person we
   can reach own a budget line?), reachability (can the founder reach 50 of them in 30 days through
   channels they already have?), wtp_comparables (do they already pay for adjacent tools?),
   competition_density (5 = sparse), unfair_advantage (founder edge in this vertical), cycle_length
   (5 = buys in days, 1 = quarters). Give a one-line evidence note per cell where possible and a total.
3. Pick the **beachhead** and justify; state the **bowling-pin sequence** (next 2 segments that reuse
   the same channel or reference customers).
4. Write the **ICP profile** for the beachhead: behavioural label (e.g. "owner-operator clinic manager
   who personally handles the schedule"), firmographics only where causal, trigger events that make
   them buy now, buyer role vs user role vs approver (PDLC Research Coverage), current workaround,
   disqualifiers (who looks like ICP but will not buy), watering holes (where 50+ of them can be reached:
   named communities, newsletters, events, associations, directories, LinkedIn filters).
5. **Excluded segments** and why (keeps focus honest).
6. Flag which ICP traits are OBSERVED (sourced) vs INFERRED.

## Required sections

## Scoring criteria
beachhead_clarity (one segment clearly dominates the scorecard), pain_urgency (for the beachhead),
budget_authority (identifiable buyer with budget), reachability (founder can get 50 conversations in
30 days without ads), segment_homogeneity (members share the workflow so one product fits).

## Kill flags for this stage
`no_reachable_segment`, `buyer_has_no_budget`, `segments_too_heterogeneous`
